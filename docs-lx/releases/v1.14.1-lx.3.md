The remaining fingerprints — `ios`, `edge`, `android`, `360`, `qq` — still carry no such key share anywhere upstream and stay rejected by new Xray; substituting them is the applications' job. Configuration, the wire format and the tag sets of desktop, router and AAR builds are unchanged.
